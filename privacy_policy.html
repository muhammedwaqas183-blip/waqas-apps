Privacy Policy

Drive Safe Islamic Edition  |  Waqas Apps

Last updated: July 2026

✅ Drive Safe Islamic Edition does not collect, store, or share any personal data from its users.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Drive Safe Islamic Edition, developed by Waqas Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use our mobile application on Android devices.

By using Drive Safe Islamic Edition, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Location data (GPS is used locally on your device only — never sent to any server)
  • Bluetooth device information
  • Usage data or analytics
  • Device identifiers
  • Financial or payment information

3. Permissions Used

Our app requests the following permissions — all used locally on your device only:

  • Bluetooth: To detect your car's Bluetooth connection and trigger the Islamic greeting automatically
  • Location: To calculate accurate Namaz (prayer) timings and Qibla direction based on your current location. Location data is never transmitted outside your device.
  • Notifications: To send prayer time reminders and Azan alerts
  • Background Service: To continue Bluetooth detection, speed monitoring, and prayer alerts when the app is minimized
  • Microphone: For the voice assistant feature (speech recognition is processed on-device)

4. Data Storage

All data (such as your name, language preference, home location, speed camera locations, and trip history) is stored locally on your device only using Android's SharedPreferences and local storage. This data is never uploaded to any server or shared with any third party.

5. Third-Party Services

Drive Safe Islamic Edition does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or data collection services. The app works fully offline for all its core features.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13 or any user of any age.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
